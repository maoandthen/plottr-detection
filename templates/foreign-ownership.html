04 · Methodology

How "foreign ownership" is defined.

Definition. A title is classified as foreign-owned where its proprietor entity is incorporated outside the United Kingdom, as recorded in HMLR's OCOD register or Companies House overseas company filings. UK-incorporated subsidiaries of foreign parents are not classified as foreign for this view, even where the ultimate beneficial owner sits offshore — that view is on the Beneficial Ownership Trace page.

Tax-haven flagging. Plottr applies the EU non-cooperative jurisdictions list and the OECD Common Reporting Standard non-participants list, supplemented by the Tax Justice Network Financial Secrecy Index top quartile. The label is descriptive, not pejorative — many legitimate businesses incorporate in these jurisdictions for non-tax reasons.

UBO data. Where shown as "disclosed", the ultimate beneficial owner has been filed under the UK Register of Overseas Entities (RoE), introduced August 2022. Many older overseas holdings have not yet filed; those are shown as undisclosed pending filing.

Onward use. This view is provided for analytical and journalistic purposes under HMLR's Use Agreement and may not be redistributed as raw data, used for direct marketing to proprietors, or relied upon for legal due diligence. Source attributions and methodology must accompany any republication.
