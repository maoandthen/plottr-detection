06 · Methodology & sources

How this profile is built.

Data sources. HM Land Registry CCOD (UK companies that own property in England and Wales), HM Land Registry OCOD (overseas companies), Companies House bulk product data (snapshot, PSC register), and HMRC trust register declarations where applicable. Updated monthly from primary sources; titles refreshed daily from HMLR delta files.

Entity matching. Plottr applies deterministic matching on company registration numbers across HMLR proprietor records and Companies House registers. Where an HMLR proprietor cannot be matched to a registered company (typical for older overseas-held titles) it is shown as unmatched and not aggregated into the headline figures.

Estimated portfolio value. The {{ estate.estimated_value|format_currency }} figure is an indicative composite based on (a) declared transaction prices recorded against titles since 2010 where available, (b) postcode-level price benchmarks for properties without disclosed price, and (c) tenure adjustments. This is a research estimate, not a valuation.

Licence and use. HMLR data is supplied to Plottr under the HM Land Registry Use Agreement. Onward use is restricted: this profile may not be used for direct marketing to the proprietors, redistributed as raw data, or relied upon for legal due diligence. For full terms see plottr.co.uk/terms.
